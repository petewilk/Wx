About this map

Colors come from the Utah Avalanche Center (UAC) overall danger rose for the day shown. Each spot is colored by its elevation band and the direction its slope faces. Slope-weighted fades the color in between 25° and 35° slopes; All terrain colors everything except nearly flat ground. Hatched stripes mean “pockets of” the next danger level up. The bar below the map plays the forecast days in a chosen range. The data are sample archived forecasts, not for travel decisions. See the current UAC forecast ↗.
