Meteogram Dashboard

Multi-panel forecast meteograms for the places you pick · temperature, humidity, precipitation, freezing level, clouds and wind · Open-Meteo
Precipitation bars are liquid-water equivalent: snow is drawn as its melted water, and the readout shows snow depth (Open-Meteo assumes about 0.7 cm of snow per mm of water). Beyond 7 days the data are shown as 3-hour values (precipitation summed, precipitation probability at its maximum, other variables averaged). At 14 and 16 days the shaded bands are the 10th–90th percentile spread of the 31-member NOAA GEFS ensemble. The red dashed line is the current time. Times are each place’s local time; the crosshair marks the same instant on every chart.
